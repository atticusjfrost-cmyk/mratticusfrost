Let me tell you something: I was driving down this long black road. It was a looooong road, going like waves. As I was riding down in my Cadillac the rain would beat down on my roof and it was like I had a little marching band of my own. And all the while I could hear a kind of humming. And I thought that if I’d follow that humming it would lead me to you. I decided that I will keep cowboying on west until I reach the mines where I’d try my hand at panning for gold. I’d look up into my rear-view mirror, and I’d see those miles spin out long behind me, and then I’d look back straight well that old black road just kept on stretching on. And the rain would be stretched and streaked across my windshield before it would pool in the cowl of the car below. And still I had this humming all around me and I wondered how much longer before this humming were to hit the right note and shatter me from the inside out. And I’d look at your empty seat beside me. Your keepsake is there, beneath it. And let me tell you: there were tears in my eyes.
